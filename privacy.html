Privacy Policy

LiveDrop · Effective November 15, 2026

LiveDrop has no accounts, no sign-in, and no developer-run servers. Your photos travel only between your own devices, and the developer cannot see them or their contents.

What we don't collect

  • Information that identifies you, such as your name, email address, or phone number
  • The photos and videos you transfer, or details such as their location data
  • Usage data from advertising or analytics tools. The app includes none

Transfers between devices

  • Photos move directly between your two devices over the same Wi-Fi or a cable. They never go over the internet
  • Wi-Fi connections are encrypted with a key set up through the first QR scan, so other devices on the network can't read them
  • Information about Macs you've connected to is stored only in your iPhone's keychain

Permissions

  • Photos: to read the photos you send to your Mac, and to save photos you receive from your Mac to the Photos app
  • Camera (iPhone, iPad): only to scan the connection QR code on your Mac's screen. No other photos are taken or saved
  • Camera (Mac): macOS requires this permission to read photos from an iPhone or iPad connected by cable. The Mac's camera is never used
  • Local Network: to find and connect to your other device on the same Wi-Fi

Purchases

The unlock purchase is handled by Apple. The developer never receives your card number or payment details, and the app only checks on your device whether the unlock has been purchased.

Storage and deletion

Photos you receive are saved in the vault folder you chose on your Mac, or in the Photos app on your iPhone, and stay there until you delete them. Deleting the app does not delete the photos in your vault folder. The developer keeps no copies.

Children

This app is not directed at children. It does not ask for age or collect information about children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the effective date above.
