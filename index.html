FAQ

What do I need?
The LiveDrop Mac app on your Mac and the LiveDrop app on your iPhone or iPad. Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi, or connect them with a cable.
How do I connect the first time?
Open "Transfer with App" in the Mac app to show a QR code, then scan it with the iPhone app. After that, the Mac appears in your list and you can pick it directly.
My Mac can't find my iPhone.
Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi. On your iPhone, check that LiveDrop is turned on in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network. If it still doesn't work, connect with a cable and use "Import via Cable" in the Mac app.
Where are my photos saved?
In the vault folder you choose on your Mac, which can also be on an external drive. Photos travel only between your two devices and never pass through any other server.
Is it free?
You can transfer for free 5 times. A one-time purchase in the Mac app unlocks unlimited transfers. If you switch Macs, use "Restore Purchases" to get it back.
